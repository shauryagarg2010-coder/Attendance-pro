Privacy Policy

Attendance Manager · Last updated: August 2026

Attendance Manager ("the app") is built for small businesses to track employee attendance, salary, and stock. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

What stays on your device

All of your business data — employee records, attendance, advances, salary calculations, stock items, and transactions — is stored locally in your browser on your own device. None of this is ever sent to, or stored on, any server. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser's site data, this information is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us, because we never had a copy of it.

What we do collect

If you sign in with Google, or use the app in guest mode, a small account record is created to keep the app working reliably and to understand overall usage. This record contains:

  • Your name and email address (only if you sign in with Google)
  • Your business name and the icon you chose for it
  • Whether you're using guest mode or a signed-in account
  • How many employees you have on record (just the count, not their names or details)
  • When you joined, and the open and close time of your most recent session
  • The app version you're using

We do not collect or transmit employee names, attendance records, salary figures, advances, stock items, or any other business data you enter — that stays on your device as described above.

Sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, that sign-in is handled entirely by Google's own authentication system, subject to Google's privacy policy. We only receive your basic profile name and email from that process.

Advertising and third parties

The app shows no ads. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

Data deletion

You can delete all locally stored business data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data for it. To request deletion of your account record (name, email, business name) from our systems, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
